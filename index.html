Privacy Policy

Ichor & Ivory, by Tortoisaur Games · Effective 25 October 2026

Short version: Ichor & Ivory does not collect, send or share any personal data.

What the game stores

The game keeps your save progress and your settings on your own device. On Android, they live in the app's private storage. In the browser version, they live in your browser's storage. Nothing leaves your device. If you uninstall the app or clear your browser data, the game deletes them.

What the game does not do

  • It does not connect to the internet. The Android app does not ask for the internet permission.
  • It has no accounts, no sign-in and no in-app purchases.
  • It shows no ads and contains no advertising or analytics code.
  • It does not read your contacts, location, camera, microphone, files or device identifiers.

Stores and platforms

Google Play and itch.io deliver the game to you. They may collect data under their own policies, which we do not control: the Google Privacy Policy and the itch.io Privacy Policy.

Children

The game collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will post the new version on this page with a new effective date.
